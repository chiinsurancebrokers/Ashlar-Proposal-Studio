# v0.4.8 — Applicant-aware client reporting

Key rules added to the client deliverable engine:

1. Sex-specific relevance: maternity/pregnancy/newborn content appears only for a female applicant.
2. Advanced imaging is diagnostic cover, not wellness. MRI/CT/PET stays separate from preventive benefits.
3. IMG Silver diagnostics: CT/PET/MRI inpatient/day-patient cover and outpatient diagnostic cover within the outpatient limit are surfaced separately from the EUR 250 well-being allowance.
4. Zero cost share is not treated as a decision point; client outputs show the deductible/excess for simplicity unless a non-zero member share is actually applicable.
5. Underwriting may be broker-overridden per option. Use Full Medical Underwriting when this is the known basis for the quoted case.
6. Important considerations are client-facing only: relevant waiting periods, material limits/sub-limits, selected area, deductible/excess, confirmed exclusions and actual use conditions.
7. Internal broker housekeeping such as "the broker must clarify", visa checks, provider-directory tasks and missing-document requests is excluded from the client report.
8. No invented healthcare-cost scenarios, visit-count estimates or probability statements.
